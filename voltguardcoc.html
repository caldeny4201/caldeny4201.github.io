Questions agents ask.

How fast can you get to a listing?

We aim for same-day certification for single-phase homes booked before 10am, subject to availability, access and compliance. Priority is aimed at within 24 hours, standard is usually 2 to 3 days, and 3-phase is usually 3 to 5 days. Same-day slots are limited, so book early.

What if the property fails?

You get a written, itemised defect list with photos and a fixed price. We start only after you approve, and the seller can use their own electrician if they prefer. The certificate is issued once the installation is compliant.

Does the CoC price include repairs?

No, repairs are separate and quoted on the defect list. The CoC + minor repairs bundle (R3,500) covers the certificate plus up to R2,000 of small repairs.

The seller has solar. Does that change anything?

New or altered electrical installations, including solar systems, generally need a Certificate of Compliance, and insurer or bank requirements can vary. We do solar-ready CoCs from R2,200, subject to inspection. We don't install solar.

Is the CoC price always the same?

Our prices are for a standard single-phase home with one DB board. Larger homes, extra boards, outbuildings, 3-phase, solar, difficult access or extra visits can change the price. We tell you before we come if your property looks non-standard, and the written quote is the price for the agreed scope.

Do you charge a call-out fee?

No call-out fee for a booked CoC inspection. A call-out fee may apply for late cancellations or no access (see Terms). A re-inspection after 30 days, or for a fault that isn't one we should have caught, is R350. It's free for retainer and panel agents.

Can the seller pay directly?

Yes, by agreement we can invoice the seller, buyer or agency. No deposit is needed for a CoC. Larger repairs may need one (see Terms).

What if a buyer's inspector flags something?

We re-inspect free within 30 days of our certificate. If a fault should reasonably have been caught at our inspection, we fix it free. If the fault is new since our inspection, we quote it and you approve before we start.

How do I join the retainer or panel?

WhatsApp us your agency name, suburb and number of agents. We send a short written agreement and aim to set you up the same day.
