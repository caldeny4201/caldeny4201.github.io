Questions agents ask.

How fast can you get to a listing?

Same-day if you book a single-phase home before 10am. Priority is within 24 hours, standard is 2 to 3 days, and 3-phase is usually 3 to 5 days. Same-day slots are limited, so book early.

What if the property fails?

You get a written, itemised defect list with photos and a fixed price. We start only after you approve, and the seller can use their own electrician if they prefer. The certificate is issued once the installation is compliant.

Does the CoC price include repairs?

No, repairs are separate and quoted on the defect list. The CoC + minor repairs bundle (R3,500) covers the certificate plus up to R2,000 of small repairs.

The seller has solar. Does that change anything?

Any new or altered installation, including inverters, batteries and panels, needs a Certificate of Compliance. We do solar-ready CoCs from R2,200. We don't install solar.

Do you charge a call-out fee?

No. The CoC price is the price. A re-inspection after 30 days, or for a fault that isn't one we should have caught, is R350. It's free for retainer and panel agents.

Can the seller pay directly?

Yes. We can invoice the seller, buyer or agency. Retainer and panel agents pay on completion with no deposit.

What if a buyer's inspector flags something?

We re-inspect free within 30 days. If we should have caught it, we fix it free. If the fault is new since our inspection, we quote it and you approve before we start.

How do I join the retainer or panel?

WhatsApp us your agency name, suburb and number of agents. We send a one-page agreement and set you up the same day.

Terms, payment and cancellation

Quotes and scope

  • Quotes are written, valid for 30 days and exclude VAT. Website prices, including repair prices, are indicative until confirmed in a written quote.
  • If we find something that changes the job, we stop and quote the extra work. We only proceed after your written approval (WhatsApp counts).
  • You must provide safe access to the property, DB board and meters.

Certificates

  • Work is done by a registered electrician in line with SANS 10142-1. A certificate is only issued when the installation is compliant.
  • Same-day: booked before 10am, single-phase, compliant installation. If we miss it, R250 off.
  • 3-hour guarantee: retainer and panel agents, single-phase, from the start of the inspection with access. If we miss it, the CoC fee is not charged. Repair time is excluded.
  • The minor repairs bundle covers up to R2,000 of small repairs. Anything above that, or excluded work, is quoted separately.

Payment

  • Per-CoC jobs: 100% on completion. Retainers: billed monthly in advance, per-CoC work monthly in arrears. Larger repairs: 50% deposit, 50% on completion unless agreed otherwise.
  • Agent Launch packs are paid upfront and the CoCs can be used any time.
  • EFT or card. Banking details appear only on your quote or invoice, so confirm by phone if anything looks unusual.

Warranty

  • 12 months on labour and parts. Not covered: lightning, surges, flooding, misuse, work altered by others.

Cancellation

  • Rescheduling is free with 24 hours' notice. Later changes or no access on arrival may incur a call-out fee of R650 to R850.
  • Retainer and panel plans are month-to-month with one month's written notice. Records are returned within 10 business days.

Limits

  • We aren't liable for pre-existing faults we weren't asked to inspect, or for indirect losses such as delayed transfers. Liability is limited to fees paid for the job, except where the law doesn't allow it. Your rights under the Consumer Protection Act are unaffected.
