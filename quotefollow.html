Questions

Before you start.

Will you contact my customers without asking me?

No. You choose which customers we may contact and by which channel, and you approve the wording first. Where permission or a lawful basis is missing, we do not send the message.

Do you guarantee I will win more jobs?

No, and we would be wary of anyone who does. We guarantee the follow-up work and the reporting. Whether a customer buys depends on your quote and your business.

What counts as a quote and a follow-up?

One quote is one priced proposal to one customer. One follow-up is one approved message or contact attempt. The pilot covers up to 15 quotes and up to two follow-ups each. Exact definitions are in the written terms.

What do I need to give you?

A list of open quotes with the date, a reference and the contact you authorise us to approach. We ask for the minimum we need and nothing more.

Why is the pilot paid?

A paid pilot keeps both sides serious and funds the time spent on your quotes. The scope is small and fixed, and you can walk away afterwards.

What happens after the 14 days?

You get the report and decide. There is no automatic renewal. If the volume justifies it, we can discuss the monthly service.

Do I need to change my software?

No. Send us your open quotes from a spreadsheet, WhatsApp, email or your job-management app. If your software already chases every quote reliably, you may not need us, and we will say so.

Do I deal with a person or a system?

A person. QuoteFollow is founder-led, so you deal directly with whoever is doing the work. Anything that needs judgement about price, technical advice or a commitment is passed to you.

Where do you work?

We work remotely for contractors in Gauteng. Onboarding and reporting are done by phone, WhatsApp and email.

How is my customers' information handled?

It is used only to follow up your quotes, kept to the minimum, and deleted or returned at the end of the service when you ask, subject to the law. The written agreement sets out the details, including how to raise a privacy request.
