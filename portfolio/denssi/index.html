How do I use them?

Place one pouch between your gum and upper lip. Do not chew or swallow it. Remove after use and bin it. Full directions are on the tin.

How much is too much?

Health authorities generally suggest up to about 400mg of caffeine a day for healthy adults, across all sources (coffee, tea, cola, energy drinks, pouches). Start with one pouch and see how you feel. .
