      FAQPricing, ownership, Smart Tools, cancelling and more+
      Is it really a once-off price?+

      Yes. The website package is a fixed, once-off fee. The only ongoing cost is hosting, which is included for 3 months (Launch) or 12 months (Growth and Dominate) and then moves to a Care plan from R299 a month.

      What do you need from me to start?+

      Your business name, logo, rate card, trade licence details, public liability insurance figures and the areas you serve. Your 5-day clock starts once we have your 50% deposit and that asset pack.

      What are Smart Tools?+

      They are the interactive tools on your site: a CoC request tool, a solar and backup picker and an itemised quote builder. Customers tap what they need, see an indicative price range, and the details land on your WhatsApp ready to quote. They're built from your own prices. The quote builder is in beta, so ask us about timing.

      Who owns the website and the domain?+

      You own your domain, logo, photos, copy and business content. Voltguard Web retains ownership of its underlying templates and source system. The site itself, including the source code and design templates, stays with Voltguard Web, and you get a licence to use it while your hosting is active. If you leave, you get 30 days to export your files. Brochures are yours outright once paid.

      What does "exclusive" mean?+

      We won't deploy the exact same visual template to two competing electricians in the same primary suburb. We keep a register of which suburb each design is used in and check it before we agree yours. Your content is always unique to you.

      How many changes do I get?+

      Two full rounds of changes before go-live, then 30 days of free fixes for genuine faults (2 months of tweaks on Dominate). After that, a Care plan covers your edits.

      What if I'm late sending my assets or approvals?+

      The timeline extends without penalty to us and the R500 late credit doesn't apply. We'll tell you straight away if we're waiting on something.

      Can I cancel a Care plan?+

      Yes, with 30 days' written notice by WhatsApp or email. There's no lock-in and no cancellation fee. Annual prepay is billed once a year in advance.

      Do you run my Google Ads or social media?+

      Not in the standard packages. Google Ads setup and management can be quoted as an add-on, and ad spend is always paid directly by you. Social media management, e-commerce, native apps and videography are quoted separately.

      Will the site get me to number one on Google?+

      We build it properly for local search, but no one can promise rankings. What we do promise is a fast, clear site that makes it easy for visitors to book you on WhatsApp.

      Can I get a refund?+

      Deposits are non-refundable once work has officially commenced. If we miss the 5-business-day approval window, you get R500 off your balance. Your rights under the Consumer Protection Act apply in full.

      Do prices include VAT?+

      Prices on this page are in South African Rand. We confirm the final amount, including any VAT that applies, on your invoice before you pay.
