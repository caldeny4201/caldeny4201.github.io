How it works

  1. Message us

    Send the address and your transfer timeline.

  2. Written quote

    You see our coordination fee and the specialist’s price, line by line. Nothing is booked until you approve.

  3. Inspection and certificate

    A registered specialist inspects, and issues the certificate under their own registration if the installation passes.

  4. Delivered to you and the conveyancer

    The certificate goes to both of you. Electrical certificates are valid for two years, and we help you time the inspection so it’s still valid on the day of registration.

Tip: book the inspection when you list, not when the offer arrives. Any defects then surface while there’s still time to fix them, not in the last week before transfer.

Questions

Are you the electricians?

No. We coordinate. A registered specialist does the inspection and any repairs, and issues the certificate under their own registration. Our job is the quote, booking, communication and payment, so you deal with one person.

Who is responsible for the certificate?

The registered specialist who issues it carries the technical responsibility. We are responsible for our process: quoting, booking, updates and the guarantees above.

How fast can you book?

We aim to confirm a specialist and slot quickly. Retainer clients get it within 2 working hours. Inspection dates depend on specialist availability, so tell us your deadline early.

Can you guarantee the property will pass?

No, and nobody honest can. If it fails, you get a photo-backed defect list and a written repair price first.

Do I need the retainer?

No. Pay-per-job has no commitment. The retainer pays for itself at about two properties a month because it waives the R450 fee.

Who pays for the certificate?

Usually the seller. Tell us who to invoice, the seller or the agency, and the quote will show it. The retainer is for agents and offices who want priority booking and the guarantees across all their listings.

Do you do gas, electric fence, plumbing or beetle certificates?

Not yet. We only do electrical certificates, so we can do them properly. Message us if you need others and we’ll tell you honestly whether we can help.

How long is the certificate valid?

An electrical certificate is generally valid for two years for transfer purposes. Tell us your expected registration date and we’ll help you time the inspection.

Do you work outside your listed suburbs?

We’re keeping our area tight so we can be reliable. Message us and we’ll tell you honestly if we can help.
