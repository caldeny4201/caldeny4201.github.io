One request. One point of contact. A clear record of what was done.

SiteCare coordinates routine maintenance for small commercial properties in Gauteng. Send us the issue, review the written quote, approve the work and receive a completion record with photos where appropriate.

No work without your approval. No unapproved spending.

How it works

  • Send the request — tell us what needs attention and where.
  • Review the quote — we coordinate with a suitable contractor.
  • Approve the work — nothing proceeds without your approval.
  • Track completion — we follow up and provide a job record.

Guarantees and trust

We commit to what we control.

Clear commitments, written down. We do not guarantee that every contractor will arrive immediately or every repair will succeed first time.
Approval before spending No contractor work or expenditure without your documented approval, except a separately agreed emergency protocol.
Quote transparency Written quote and clear exclusions before approval.
Communication Acknowledge requests during business hours within an agreed target, such as four business hours.
Job records Record the approved scope, status, contractor and completion evidence.
Contractor follow-up Follow up on missed appointments and unresolved issues.
Service recovery Consider a defined service credit if SiteCare misses a commitment within its control.

We check insurance, contracts, contractor vetting, data protection and legal responsibilities. Describing SiteCare as a coordinator does not automatically remove liability.

Who we serve

Built for small commercial portfolios.

We start with property managers responsible for approximately 3–30 small commercial properties.
  • Independent property managers: coordinate repairs across multiple properties and value reliable updates, quote approval and a central maintenance register.
  • Commercial landlords: owners of small retail, office and mixed-use properties who do not have an in-house facilities team.
  • Later: multi-site small businesses: retailers, agencies and other businesses operating several premises that need a consistent maintenance process.
  • Not for us (yet): large, complex facilities contracts requiring extensive compliance documentation, formal procurement and service-level commitments.

Promotions and incentives

Simple ways to start.

New-customer deal: First qualifying coordination fee waived (save R495).
Low-risk entry: Free maintenance workflow review.
Pilot offer: One month with a defined request limit and no long-term commitment.

First 30 days

A controlled launch.

  • Create request form, quote approval process

Week 3 — First job

  • Contact 40–50 prospects
  • Offer workflow reviews
  • Secure a first paid or promotional pilot

Week 4 — Measure & refine

  • Record time spent per job
  • Track customer satisfaction & reliability
  • Adjust scope and pricing before scaling
