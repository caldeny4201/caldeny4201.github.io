Terms of Engagement

Last updated: January 2025 · Applies to: all SiteCare engagements within the Republic of South Africa.

This is a summary of our standard engagement terms. A full written engagement letter is issued to every client before work begins and takes precedence where it differs from this summary.

1. Nature of the Service

SiteCare provides remote maintenance coordination services for retail property managers. SiteCare's role is to receive maintenance requests, obtain written quotes from vetted contractors, present those quotes for client approval, coordinate the approved work, follow up with contractors, and provide completion records with photographic evidence where appropriate.

SiteCare does not perform the physical maintenance work. Independent contractors perform all physical work. SiteCare coordinates, documents, and follows up. SiteCare never adds a markup to contractor work — contractors invoice the client directly at their own rate.

2. Scope of Services

The specific services, coordination request limits, deadlines, and deliverables for each client are recorded in a written engagement letter and confirmed at the start of the engagement. Services not expressly included in the engagement letter are excluded.

Not included, and quoted separately where applicable:

  • Physical maintenance work of any kind
  • Materials and parts
  • Specialist work beyond routine repairs (structural, major electrical, regulated work)
  • Compliance documentation beyond the completion record
  • Insurance claims coordination
  • Formal procurement processes
  • After-hours emergencies beyond the signed Emergency Protocol

3. Client Responsibilities

The client agrees to:

  • Provide clear descriptions of maintenance requests, including property, site contact, and urgency
  • Respond to quote approval requests within a reasonable time
  • Provide site access and reasonable cooperation for approved work
  • Notify SiteCare promptly of any change in property portfolio, site contacts, or trade requirements
  • Sign the Emergency Protocol at onboarding, before any monthly service begins
  • Pay contractors directly for approved work at the contractor's stated rate
  • Maintain the security of any credentials or access information shared with SiteCare

4. One Request — Defined

For the purposes of monthly request limits:

  • One request is one reported issue at one property, requiring one contractor visit and one written quote
  • Multiple issues at the same property on the same day count as separate requests, unless a single contractor visit resolves them all
  • Follow-up visits for the same issue do not count as a new request
  • Scope changes requiring a new quote count as a new request
  • Emergency call-outs are billed separately under the Emergency Protocol

5. Emergency Protocol

Every client must sign an Emergency Protocol before any monthly service begins. The protocol records:

  • Named emergency contacts for the client (minimum two)
  • Pre-authorised spend limit for emergency coordination
  • Definition of what qualifies as an emergency
  • Escalation path if the client cannot be reached
  • Billing terms for after-hours call-outs

No emergency coordination will be performed before the Emergency Protocol is signed and on file.

6. Contractor Network

Contractors in the SiteCare network are vetted against a published standard: business registration, public liability insurance, relevant trade qualification where required, two contactable references, written acceptance of service terms, and agreement to communication standards. Contractors are reviewed annually, and immediately on service failure.

SiteCare does not guarantee contractor workmanship. Contractor warranties are their own. SiteCare's role is coordination, documentation, and follow-up.

7. Fees and Payment

Monthly fees are fixed and published on the pricing page at the time of engagement. Monthly plans cover a specific number of coordination requests as stated. Requests exceeding the monthly limit are quoted in writing before they begin. Emergency coordination is billed separately at the published rate.

SiteCare invoices only for its own coordination services. Contractor invoices are issued directly to the client and paid directly by the client.

8. Guarantees

SiteCare guarantees:

  • No markup: SiteCare never adds a markup to contractor work
  • Approval before spend: no work without documented client approval, except under the signed Emergency Protocol
  • Written quote transparency: written quote with clear exclusions before every approval
  • Communication: requests acknowledged within 4 business hours during business days
  • Job records: approved scope, status, contractor, and completion evidence recorded
  • Contractor follow-up: missed appointments and unresolved issues chased by SiteCare
  • Vetted contractors: every contractor confirmed against the published standard
  • Named contact: one person follows up on your jobs
  • Service recovery: reasonable service credit for missed commitments within our control
  • Month-to-month: one month's written notice, either side
What we do not guarantee: that a contractor will arrive at an exact time, that a repair will succeed on the first attempt, that every emergency can be resolved within a fixed period, that a contractor's workmanship will be perfect, or that SiteCare will carry the contractor's liability. SiteCare is a coordinator. Contractors perform the physical work.

9. Confidentiality

SiteCare treats all client information — including property details, tenant information, and contractor records — as confidential. Information is disclosed only to the client and persons the client has authorised, to service providers under written confidentiality obligations strictly necessary to deliver the service, and where required by law or a regulator with lawful authority.

10. Data Protection (POPIA)

SiteCare processes personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 4 of 2013. See the and for full details.

11. Limitation of Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by South African law:

  • SiteCare's total liability arising out of or in connection with the engagement is limited to the fees paid by the client in the three (3) calendar months immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim
  • SiteCare is not liable for indirect, incidental, special, or consequential losses
  • SiteCare is not liable for the acts or omissions of independent contractors, banks, insurers, tenants, or other third parties

Nothing in these terms limits liability for fraud, wilful misconduct, or any liability that cannot be limited by law.

12. Termination

Either party may terminate the engagement on one (1) calendar month's written notice. Outstanding jobs continue to completion. Fees for work completed up to termination remain payable. All records, completion photos, and the maintenance register are returned in an agreed format within 10 business days of final payment.

13. Dispute Resolution

Parties will first attempt good-faith resolution within 30 days. If unresolved, the dispute will be referred to mediation and, failing that, to arbitration under the rules of the Arbitration Foundation of Southern Africa (AFSA). Nothing prevents either party from seeking urgent interim relief from a competent court.

14. Governing Law

15. Amendments

Emergency Protocol

Last updated: January 2025

Every SiteCare client must sign an Emergency Protocol before any monthly service begins. No emergency coordination will be performed before the signed protocol is on file. This is not optional.

1. Why this protocol exists

Emergencies are where service businesses get sued. A burst pipe at 2am, a broken shopfront, a power failure on a Saturday — these situations require clear rules agreed in advance, not improvised decisions under pressure. The Emergency Protocol protects both parties.

2. What the protocol records

  • Named emergency contacts: minimum two contacts per client, with after-hours phone numbers
  • Pre-authorised spend limit: the amount SiteCare may authorise without further approval, typically R5,000
  • Definition of emergency: what qualifies (burst pipe, power failure, broken glass, security breach, flooding) and what does not
  • Escalation path: what happens if the primary contact cannot be reached, and who authorises next
  • Site access: how contractors reach the site after hours, and who unlocks
  • Billing terms: R1,500 per after-hours call-out, billed separately from contractor charges

3. What SiteCare does in an emergency

  • Contacts the appropriate vetted contractor
  • Coordinates the emergency response within the pre-authorised spend limit
  • Documents the call-out, the work performed, and the completion evidence
  • Notifies the client as soon as is reasonably possible
  • Bills the emergency call-out separately from the monthly plan

4. What SiteCare does not do

  • Authorise spend beyond the pre-agreed limit without client approval
  • Perform emergency work itself
  • Carry the contractor's liability
  • Guarantee a fixed response time in all circumstances

5. Getting the protocol signed

The Emergency Protocol is provided at onboarding and must be signed before the first monthly coordination request is processed. It is reviewed annually, and immediately on any change in site contacts, ownership, or portfolio.

1. How to raise a complaint

Email complaints@sitecare.co.za with:

  • Your name and portfolio
  • The date the issue arose
  • A short description of the problem
  • The outcome you are seeking

2. What happens next

  • Within 1 business day: we acknowledge your complaint
  • Within 5 business days: we provide an initial response
  • Within 20 business days: we provide a substantive response, or an explanation of why more time is needed

3. Escalation

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may escalate in writing to the owner. If the matter remains unresolved, you may refer it to:

  • Mediation or arbitration under our
  • The Information Regulator of South Africa, for personal information matters
  • A competent court, for any other legal remedy

4. Service recovery

Where we have missed a commitment within our control, we may offer a reasonable service credit. The decision is made on the facts of each case.

5. Record keeping

We keep a record of all complaints, our responses, and the resolution, for quality improvement and for statutory purposes.
