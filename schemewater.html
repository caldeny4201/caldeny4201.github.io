The problem

When water equipment fails, trustees need clear records and a practical plan.

Many Gauteng properties now depend on their own tanks, pumps or boreholes. BusinessTech reported households spending between R15,000 and R120,000 on them. The Borehole Water Association estimates Gauteng residents drilled about 10,000 boreholes in the past decade, as reported by Reuters. Once installed, the equipment still needs looking after.

Thin records

It can be hard to say when the tanks were last cleaned or the pump last serviced.

Repairs arrive as surprises

A fault can become an emergency call-out and a quote the trustees have little time to compare.

Trustees need a plan on paper

Sectional-title schemes are expected to keep a 10-year maintenance plan and report on it to owners each year. Dated water-system records give the managing agent something real to put in it.

The report

A report trustees can circulate and act on.

The report is the product. It gives the managing agent a record, and the trustees a clear list of what to decide.

In every report

  • Equipment register: tanks, pumps, valves and backup systems.
  • Dated photographs and observations.
  • OK, Watch or Act status for each item.
  • Prioritised recommended actions.
  • Written quotes where you ask for them.
  • Maintenance history and next inspection dates.
  • Limitations, including anything we could not inspect.

What the labels mean

  • OK: no action needed at the time of inspection.
  • Watch: not urgent. Monitor it or plan for it.
  • Act: a fix is recommended soon. The report says what we saw and the suggested next step.

A status label is an observation. It is not an engineering certificate.

Questions

Before you book.

Who does the physical work?

A registered plumbing contractor works under a written scope. We coordinate the visit, write the report and manage the quotes.

Does this replace our 10-year maintenance plan?

No. That plan is prepared by a building maintenance professional. Our report gives you dated, photographed records of the water equipment to feed into it.

Do you replace our current maintenance contractor?

Not necessarily. The check gives you a record and quotes you can compare. You can use your current contractor for any repair.

What does R1,950 cover, and what changes the price?

A defined inspection and report for up to 2 tanks and 1 pump set on one site in our service area. Larger schemes, longer travel or difficult access are quoted. Water sampling is an optional add-on priced on request. The fee is credited against your first care-plan month if you start within 30 days. Pricing is indicative until confirmed in writing.

Does this fix municipal outages?

No. It shows how well your own storage, pumps and backup supply will cope when the City supply fails.

Do you cover solar and other systems?

Not yet. We start with water so we can do one thing properly. Ask us if you have another system you are worried about.

I manage several schemes. Can you do all of them?

We start with one scheme so you can see the report and the quotes. If it is useful, we agree pricing for the rest of your portfolio afterwards.

Which areas do you cover?

Sandton, Bryanston, Fourways, Rivonia and Randburg. Ask us about schemes nearby.
