Terms of Engagement

Last updated: January 2025 · Applies to: all LedgerCare engagements within the Republic of South Africa.

This is a summary of our standard engagement terms. A full written engagement letter is issued to every client before work begins and takes precedence where it differs from this summary.

1. Scope of Services

LedgerCare provides remote monthly bookkeeping, bank reconciliation, trust account reconciliation, document organisation, and reporting services to small law firms. The specific services, transaction limits, deadlines, and deliverables for each client are recorded in a written engagement letter and confirmed at the start of the engagement.

Services not expressly included in the engagement letter are excluded. These typically include, but are not limited to:

  • Payroll processing and PAYE/UIF/SDL submissions
  • VAT returns and VAT reconciliations
  • Annual financial statements and independent reviews
  • Income tax returns and SARS submissions of any kind
  • Catch-up bookkeeping for prior periods
  • Audit, assurance, or attest work
  • Legal Practice Council compliance submissions or trust account audits
  • Complex multi-entity consolidations

These services are quoted separately in writing, accepted by the client, and billed independently of the monthly retainer.

2. Client Responsibilities

Accurate, timely bookkeeping depends on accurate, timely source information. The client agrees to:

  • Provide complete records, bank and trust statements, and supporting documents by the agreed monthly deadline
  • Respond to requests for missing or unclear information within a reasonable time
  • Review and approve all reports before relying on them for any decision
  • Notify LedgerCare promptly of any change in practice structure, banking, trust account arrangements, VAT status, or accounting system
  • Maintain the security of any credentials shared with LedgerCare and rotate them on request

Where records are incomplete or arrive late, LedgerCare may adjust delivery dates without penalty.

3. Scope Definitions

For the purposes of monthly transaction limits:

  • One transaction is one line item in the bank statement or ledger. Where a single bank entry represents multiple underlying items, it counts as one transaction at the bank level, but the underlying items still need to be supported by documentation.
  • One bank account is one account at one financial institution. Trust and business accounts at the same bank count separately.
  • One entity is one registered company, close corporation, partnership, or sole proprietor. Multi-entity consolidation is quoted separately.
  • Work beyond scope is any request requiring more than 30 minutes of additional time. It is quoted in writing before it begins.

4. Trust Account Work

Where trust account reconciliation is included in the engagement, LedgerCare will reconcile the trust bank account against the trust ledger on the frequency agreed in the engagement letter. This is a bookkeeping reconciliation and is not a Legal Practice Council trust audit, an assurance engagement, or a substitute for the practice's own statutory obligations.

The client remains responsible for all Legal Practice Council compliance obligations, including any trust account audit required by law.

5. Fees and Payment

Monthly fees are fixed and published on the pricing page at the time of engagement. The client agrees to pay by the agreed method on or before the invoice due date. Fees cover the specified monthly deliverables and transaction limits. Work exceeding agreed limits is quoted in writing before it begins.

6. Guarantees

LedgerCare guarantees:

  • Clear scope: inclusions, exclusions, and transaction limits are written down before work starts
  • Trust account work: performed by staff with specific experience in legal trust accounting, under supervision of a qualified accounting professional
  • Fixed monthly fee: no hourly billing for work within scope
  • No surprise charges: additional work requires a written quote and client approval
  • Delivery commitment: monthly deliverables completed by the agreed date if records arrive by the agreed deadline
  • Correction policy: errors attributable to LedgerCare are corrected at no additional fee
  • Communication: requests acknowledged within one business day
  • Cancellation: one month's written notice, either side. All records returned within 10 business days of final payment
  • Service recovery: a reasonable service credit for a missed commitment within our control
What we do not guarantee: perfect books, zero tax problems, guaranteed SARS outcomes, Legal Practice Council compliance outcomes, audit-ready financial statements without a separate engagement, or recovery of outstanding debtor balances. LedgerCare is not a substitute for a registered tax practitioner, registered auditor, trust account auditor, or the client's own professional judgment.

7. Confidentiality

LedgerCare treats all client financial, trust, and personal information as confidential. Information is disclosed only to the client and persons the client has authorised in writing, to service providers under written confidentiality obligations strictly necessary to deliver the service, and where required by law or a regulator with lawful authority.

This obligation survives termination of the engagement, and includes the practice's clients' information and any information subject to legal professional privilege.

8. Data Protection (POPIA)

LedgerCare processes personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 4 of 2013. See the and for full details.

9. Limitation of Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by South African law:

  • LedgerCare's total liability arising out of or in connection with the engagement is limited to the fees paid by the client in the three (3) calendar months immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim
  • LedgerCare is not liable for indirect, incidental, special, or consequential losses
  • LedgerCare is not liable for the acts or omissions of third parties, including banks, SARS, the Legal Practice Council, accounting software providers, or independent professionals engaged with the client's written consent

Nothing in these terms limits liability for fraud, wilful misconduct, or any liability that cannot be limited by law.

10. Termination

Either party may terminate the engagement on one (1) calendar month's written notice, or as otherwise provided in the engagement letter. Fees for work completed up to the termination date remain payable. On termination, LedgerCare will return or securely destroy client records as instructed, subject to statutory retention obligations.

11. Dispute Resolution

Parties will first attempt good-faith resolution within 30 days. If unresolved, the dispute will be referred to mediation and, failing that, to arbitration under the rules of the Arbitration Foundation of Southern Africa (AFSA). Nothing prevents either party from seeking urgent interim relief from a competent court.

12. Governing Law

These terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of South Africa.

13. Amendments

LedgerCare may amend these terms from time to time. Material changes will be communicated to clients in writing at least fourteen (14) days before taking effect.
