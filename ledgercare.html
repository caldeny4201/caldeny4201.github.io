Medical aid remittance reconciliation

Medical aids pay late, pay short, and pay against different claim references than the practice expects. Reconciling remittances against the practice's claims and bank deposits is the single most time-consuming part of practice bookkeeping — and the part most likely to leave money unclaimed.

LedgerCare reconciles medical aid remittances monthly. Every payment is matched to a claim. Every shortfall is flagged. Every outstanding remittance is chased. Included in Practice Insights and Practice Partner.

Included Insights & Partner
What counts as one transaction: one line item in the bank statement or ledger — a debit or credit, a bank charge, a deposit, a payment, a medical aid remittance. Where a single bank entry represents multiple claims, it counts as one transaction at the bank level, but the underlying claims still need to be supported by documentation.
What is not included in any tier: payroll processing, VAT returns, annual financial statements, SARS submissions and tax returns, catch-up bookkeeping for prior periods, audit and assurance work, HPCSA compliance submissions, and complex multi-practice consolidation. These are quoted separately, in writing, before any work begins.

Terms of Engagement

Last updated: January 2025 · Applies to: all LedgerCare engagements within the Republic of South Africa.

This is a summary of our standard engagement terms. A full written engagement letter is issued to every client before work begins and takes precedence where it differs from this summary. LedgerCare is currently pre-launch. These terms apply from the date of the first paid engagement.

1. Scope of Services

LedgerCare provides remote monthly bookkeeping, bank reconciliation, medical aid remittance reconciliation, patient debtor tracking, document organisation, and reporting services to small physiotherapy practices. The specific services, transaction limits, deadlines, and deliverables for each client are recorded in a written engagement letter and confirmed at the start of the engagement.

Services not expressly included in the engagement letter are excluded. These typically include:

  • Payroll processing and PAYE/UIF/SDL submissions
  • VAT returns and VAT reconciliations
  • Annual financial statements and independent reviews
  • Income tax returns and SARS submissions of any kind
  • Catch-up bookkeeping for prior periods
  • Audit, assurance, or attest work
  • HPCSA compliance submissions or medical scheme audit support
  • Medical billing, claims submission, or practice management software support
  • Complex multi-practice consolidations

These services are quoted separately in writing, accepted by the client, and billed independently of the monthly retainer.

2. Client Responsibilities

Accurate, timely bookkeeping depends on accurate, timely source information. The client agrees to:

  • Provide complete records, bank statements, remittance advices, and supporting documents by the agreed monthly deadline
  • Respond to requests for missing or unclear information within a reasonable time
  • Review and approve all reports before relying on them for any decision
  • Notify LedgerCare promptly of any change in practice structure, banking, medical scheme arrangements, VAT status, or accounting system
  • Maintain the security of any credentials shared with LedgerCare and rotate them on request

Where records are incomplete or arrive late, LedgerCare may adjust delivery dates without penalty.

3. Scope Definitions

For the purposes of monthly transaction limits:

  • One transaction is one line item in the bank statement or ledger, including each medical aid remittance.
  • One bank account is one account at one financial institution. Practice and personal accounts count separately.
  • One practice is one registered entity with one HPCSA practice number. Multi-practice consolidation is quoted separately.
  • Work beyond scope is any request requiring more than 30 minutes of additional time. It is quoted in writing before it begins.

4. Medical Aid Remittance Work

Where medical aid remittance reconciliation is included in the engagement, LedgerCare will reconcile medical aid remittances against the practice's claims and bank deposits on the frequency agreed in the engagement letter. This is a bookkeeping reconciliation and is not a medical billing audit, an assurance engagement, or a substitute for the practice's own statutory obligations or medical scheme obligations.

The client remains responsible for all HPCSA, BHF, and medical scheme compliance obligations, including any billing audit or forensic review required by a medical scheme.

5. Fees and Payment

Monthly fees are fixed and published on the pricing page at the time of engagement. The client agrees to pay by the agreed method on or before the invoice due date. Fees cover the specified monthly deliverables and transaction limits. Work exceeding agreed limits is quoted in writing before it begins.

6. Guarantees

LedgerCare guarantees:

  • Clear scope: inclusions, exclusions, and transaction limits written down before work starts
  • Medical aid remittance work: performed by staff with physiotherapy practice bookkeeping experience under supervision of a qualified accounting professional
  • Fixed monthly fee: no hourly billing for work within scope
  • No surprise charges: additional work requires a written quote and client approval
  • Delivery commitment: monthly deliverables completed by the agreed date if records arrive by the agreed deadline
  • Correction policy: errors attributable to LedgerCare corrected at no additional fee
  • Communication: requests acknowledged within one business day
  • Cancellation: one month's written notice, either side. All records returned within 10 business days of final payment
  • Service recovery: a reasonable service credit for a missed commitment within our control
What we do not guarantee: perfect books, zero tax problems, guaranteed SARS outcomes, HPCSA compliance outcomes, protection against medical scheme audits, audit-ready financial statements without a separate engagement, or recovery of outstanding patient debtor balances. LedgerCare is a bookkeeping service.

7. Confidentiality

LedgerCare treats all client financial, patient, and personal information as confidential. Information is disclosed only to the client and persons the client has authorised in writing, to service providers under written confidentiality obligations strictly necessary to deliver the service, and where required by law or a regulator with lawful authority. Patient information is treated as special personal information under POPIA.

8. Data Protection (POPIA)

LedgerCare processes personal information in accordance with POPIA. See the , , and for full details.

9. Limitation of Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by South African law:

  • LedgerCare's total liability arising out of or in connection with the engagement is limited to the fees paid by the client in the three (3) calendar months immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim
  • LedgerCare is not liable for indirect, incidental, special, or consequential losses
  • LedgerCare is not liable for the acts or omissions of third parties, including banks, medical schemes, SARS, HPCSA, practice management software providers, or independent professionals engaged with the client's written consent

Nothing in these terms limits liability for fraud, wilful misconduct, or any liability that cannot be limited by law.

10. Termination

Either party may terminate the engagement on one (1) calendar month's written notice, or as otherwise provided in the engagement letter. Fees for work completed up to the termination date remain payable. On termination, LedgerCare will return or securely destroy client records as instructed, subject to statutory retention obligations.

11. Dispute Resolution

Parties will first attempt good-faith resolution within 30 days. If unresolved, the dispute will be referred to mediation and, failing that, to arbitration under the rules of the Arbitration Foundation of Southern Africa (AFSA). Nothing prevents either party from seeking urgent interim relief from a competent court.

12. Governing Law

These terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of South Africa.

13. Amendments

LedgerCare may amend these terms from time to time. Material changes will be communicated to clients in writing at least fourteen (14) days before taking effect.

Medical Data Statement

Patient information is special personal information under POPIA and requires heightened protection. This statement explains exactly what LedgerCare does — and does not do — with patient data and medical aid information. It is published so that practice owners understand our scope before they engage.

1. What we do with patient data

Where medical aid remittance reconciliation or patient debtor tracking is included in the engagement, LedgerCare:

  • Uses patient names, medical aid scheme names, claim references, and payment amounts to reconcile remittances against bank deposits
  • Tracks outstanding patient balances against ageing categories
  • Flags shortfalls, unpaid claims, and old debtors in the monthly reporting
  • Stores patient data in secure, access-controlled systems under the same standards as all other client information
  • Processes patient data only on the practice's documented instruction, as an operator

2. What we do not do

  • We do not submit medical claims to medical schemes
  • We do not perform HPCSA compliance audits
  • We do not audit billing practices or coding
  • We do not protect against medical scheme audits or forensic reviews
  • We do not certify compliance with the HPCSA, BHF, or any medical scheme
  • We do not use patient data for any purpose other than delivering the bookkeeping engagement
  • We do not share patient data with any third party except under the terms of the Privacy Policy

3. What the practice remains responsible for

  • Compliance with the HPCSA Code of Conduct and all practice standards
  • Accurate billing and coding of clinical services
  • Any medical scheme audit, forensic review, or billing query
  • Patient consent and records management for clinical purposes
  • Notifying LedgerCare immediately of any suspected data breach affecting patient information

4. POPIA and patient data

Patient information is treated as special personal information under POPIA. This means it receives heightened protection. LedgerCare processes patient data:

  • Only on the practice's documented instruction
  • Only to the extent necessary for the bookkeeping engagement
  • Only for the period necessary to deliver the service and satisfy statutory retention obligations
  • Under written confidentiality obligations from staff and contractors
  • With access controls and encryption where technically feasible

5. Data subject rights

Patients whose data is processed by LedgerCare retain all their rights under POPIA. Where a patient requests access, correction, or deletion, the practice will be the primary point of contact. LedgerCare will cooperate with the practice to fulfil the request within POPIA timeframes.

6. Security incidents

If a security compromise affects patient information, LedgerCare will notify the practice and the Information Regulator as required by section 22 of POPIA, and will cooperate fully with the practice's response.

Complaints Procedure

Last updated: January 2025

We would rather hear about a problem early than lose a client quietly. This procedure explains how to raise a concern and what you can expect from us in response.

1. How to raise a complaint

Email denysschen.callum43@gmail.com with:

  • Your name and practice
  • The date the issue arose
  • A short description of the problem
  • The outcome you are seeking

2. What happens next

  • Within 1 business day: we acknowledge your complaint
  • Within 5 business days: we provide an initial response
  • Within 20 business days: we provide a substantive response, or an explanation of why more time is needed

3. Escalation

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may escalate in writing to the owner. If the matter remains unresolved, you may refer it to:

  • Mediation or arbitration under our
  • The Information Regulator of South Africa, for personal information matters

Where we have missed a commitment within our control, we may offer a reasonable service credit.

We keep a record of all complaints, our responses, and the resolution, for quality improvement and for statutory purposes.
