Terms of Engagement

Last updated: January 2025 · Applies to: all LedgerCare engagements within the Republic of South Africa.

This is a summary of our standard engagement terms. A full written engagement letter is issued to every client before work begins and takes precedence where it differs from this summary.

1. Scope of Services

LedgerCare provides remote monthly bookkeeping, bank reconciliation, document organisation, and reporting services. The specific services, transaction limits, deadlines, and deliverables for each client are recorded in a written engagement letter and confirmed at the start of the engagement.

Services not expressly included in the engagement letter are excluded. These typically include, but are not limited to:

  • Payroll processing and PAYE/UIF/SDL submissions
  • VAT returns and VAT reconciliations
  • Annual financial statements and independent reviews
  • Income tax returns and SARS submissions of any kind
  • Catch-up bookkeeping for prior periods
  • Audit, assurance, or attest work
  • Complex multi-entity consolidations

These services are quoted separately in writing, accepted by the client, and billed independently of the monthly retainer.

2. Client Responsibilities

Accurate, timely bookkeeping depends on accurate, timely source information. The client agrees to:

  • Provide complete records, bank statements, and supporting documents by the agreed monthly deadline
  • Respond to requests for missing or unclear information within a reasonable time
  • Review and approve all reports before relying on them for any decision
  • Notify LedgerCare promptly of any change in business structure, banking, VAT status, or accounting system
  • Maintain the security of any credentials shared with LedgerCare and rotate them on request

Where records are incomplete or arrive late, LedgerCare may adjust delivery dates without penalty.

3. Fees and Payment

Monthly fees are fixed and published on the pricing page at the time of engagement. The client agrees to pay by the agreed method on or before the invoice due date. Fees cover the specified monthly deliverables and transaction limits. Work exceeding agreed limits is quoted in writing before it begins.

Once a written quote for additional work has been accepted, it becomes payable whether or not the client subsequently decides to proceed with the underlying matter, except where the engagement letter states otherwise.

4. Guarantees

LedgerCare guarantees:

  • Clear scope: inclusions, exclusions, and transaction limits are written down before work starts
  • Fixed monthly fee: no hourly billing for work within scope
  • No surprise charges: additional work requires a written quote and client approval
  • Delivery commitment: monthly deliverables completed by the agreed date if records arrive by the agreed deadline
  • Correction policy: errors attributable to LedgerCare are corrected at no additional fee
  • Communication: requests acknowledged within one business day
  • Cancellation: month-to-month after any disclosed initial setup or pilot terms
  • Service recovery: a reasonable service credit for a missed commitment within our control
What we do not guarantee: perfect books, zero tax problems, guaranteed SARS outcomes, audit-ready financial statements without a separate engagement, or recovery of outstanding debtor balances. LedgerCare is not a substitute for a registered tax practitioner, registered auditor, or the client's own professional judgment.

5. Confidentiality

LedgerCare treats all client financial and personal information as confidential. Information is disclosed only:

  • To the client and persons the client has authorised in writing
  • To service providers under written confidentiality obligations strictly necessary to deliver the service
  • Where required by law, court order, or a regulator with lawful authority

This obligation survives termination of the engagement.

6. Data Protection (POPIA)

LedgerCare processes personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 4 of 2013 ("POPIA"). See the and for full details of our processing purposes, retention, and data subject rights.

7. Limitation of Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by South African law:

  • LedgerCare's total liability arising out of or in connection with the engagement, whether in contract, delict, or otherwise, is limited to the fees paid by the client in the three (3) calendar months immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim
  • LedgerCare is not liable for indirect, incidental, special, or consequential losses, including loss of profit, loss of opportunity, or loss of data arising from client-side systems
  • LedgerCare is not liable for the acts or omissions of third parties, including banks, SARS, accounting software providers, or independent professionals engaged with the client's written consent

Nothing in these terms limits liability for fraud, wilful misconduct, or any liability that cannot be limited by law.

8. Termination

Either party may terminate the engagement on one (1) calendar month's written notice, or as otherwise provided in the engagement letter. Fees for work completed up to the termination date remain payable. On termination, LedgerCare will return or securely destroy client records as instructed, subject to statutory retention obligations.

9. Dispute Resolution

Parties will first attempt to resolve any dispute through good-faith discussion within 30 days. If unresolved, the dispute will be referred to mediation, and failing that, to arbitration in accordance with the rules of the Arbitration Foundation of Southern Africa (AFSA). This clause does not prevent either party from seeking urgent interim relief from a competent court.

10. Governing Law

These terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of South Africa. The parties consent to the jurisdiction of the South African courts.

11. Amendments

LedgerCare may amend these terms from time to time. Material changes will be communicated to clients in writing at least fourteen (14) days before taking effect. Continued use of the service after that date constitutes acceptance.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how LedgerCare ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information, and describes your rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 4 of 2013 ("POPIA") and other applicable South African law.

1. Who we are

LedgerCare is a remote bookkeeping and monthly reporting service operating in South Africa. We are the "responsible party" for the personal information we process to provide the service.

Information Officer: The owner of LedgerCare
Email: privacy@ledgercare.co.za

2. Personal information we collect

We collect information that is necessary to deliver bookkeeping and reporting services. This typically includes:

  • Identity and contact data: name, business name, registration number, email, phone number, business address
  • Financial data: bank statements, transaction records, invoices, debtor and creditor information, tax reference numbers
  • Payroll data (only where the client engages us for a separate quoted service): employee names, ID numbers, remuneration, deductions
  • Technical and usage data: IP address, browser type, timestamps, and access logs generated by our booking system and secure file transfer tools
  • Communications: emails, WhatsApp messages, meeting notes, and support tickets related to your engagement

We do not knowingly collect information from children, and we do not collect special personal information (as defined in POPIA) except where strictly necessary and lawful.

3. How we collect it

  • Directly from you, when you contact us, book a health check, or engage our services
  • From documents and records you share with us through secure channels
  • From your existing accountants or bookkeepers, where you have instructed them to hand over records
  • Automatically, through our website, booking tools, and file transfer platforms

4. Why we process it (lawful basis)

We process personal information on the following bases under POPIA:

  • To perform a contract: delivering the bookkeeping and reporting services you have engaged us for
  • To comply with legal obligations: tax, company, and record-keeping laws
  • For legitimate interests: protecting our systems, preventing fraud, improving our service quality, and communicating with you about your engagement
  • With your consent: for optional marketing communications and certain referrals

5. How we use it

  • To prepare monthly bookkeeping, reconciliations, and reports
  • To track outstanding invoices and debtor balances
  • To communicate with you about your engagement, deadlines, and missing records
  • To invoice you and manage payments
  • To comply with tax, statutory, and regulatory obligations
  • To detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, security incidents, or misuse

6. Who we share it with

We share personal information only where necessary, and only with parties bound by confidentiality:

  • Our accounting professionals: suitably qualified bookkeepers and accountants working on your engagement under written confidentiality obligations
  • Service providers: secure file storage, e-signature, and communication tools that support the service
  • Professional advisers: our own attorneys, insurers, and auditors, where necessary
  • Authorities: SARS, the Information Regulator, courts, or other regulators where we are legally compelled

We do not sell personal information. We do not share it for third-party marketing.

7. Cross-border transfers

Some of our service providers may store data outside South Africa. Where this occurs, we take reasonable steps to ensure the recipient is subject to laws, binding rules, or contractual obligations that provide an adequate level of protection substantially similar to POPIA.

8. How long we keep it

We retain personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described above, and to satisfy legal retention obligations. In practice:

  • Client engagement records: at least five (5) years after the end of the tax year to which they relate, as required by tax and company law
  • Prospective client enquiries: up to twelve (12) months if no engagement follows
  • Website and booking logs: typically up to twelve (12) months

When retention is no longer justified, information is securely destroyed or de-identified.

9. How we secure it

We take reasonable, appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, including:

  • Access control, unique credentials, and the principle of least privilege
  • Encrypted storage and encrypted transfer where technically feasible
  • Secure file-sharing platforms rather than unprotected email attachments
  • Written confidentiality obligations for staff and contractors
  • Procedures for detecting, reporting, and responding to security compromises

No system is perfectly secure. If a security compromise affects your personal information, we will notify you and the Information Regulator as required by section 22 of POPIA.

10. Your rights

Under POPIA you have the right to:

  • Be notified that we collect your personal information, and why
  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of personal information that is inaccurate, irrelevant, excessive, misleading, or unlawfully obtained
  • Object to processing on reasonable grounds
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@ledgercare.co.za. We respond within a reasonable time and, in any event, within the periods prescribed by POPIA.

11. Cookies and website analytics

Our website uses minimal cookies and similar technologies to operate the site and understand basic usage. See our for details.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version is always published on our website with a revised "last updated" date.
