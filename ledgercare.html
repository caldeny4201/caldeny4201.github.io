Delivery & compliance

A repeatable process, every client.

Before accepting a client, we establish a clear workflow.
  • Understand the business, accounts, transaction volume, and reporting needs.
  • Agree on scope, price, deadlines, and exclusions in writing.
  • Collect records through a secure, access-controlled process.
  • Have the accounting professional process and reconcile records.
  • Review the work and resolve missing information.
  • Deliver agreed reports and explain outstanding issues.
  • Invoice monthly and follow up on renewal and service quality.

We confirm relevant requirements for the actual services we sell. Tax practitioner registration, audit work and other regulated activities have specific rules. We use suitably qualified professionals, protect client financial information, and comply with POPIA.
