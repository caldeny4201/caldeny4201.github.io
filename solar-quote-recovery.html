Quotes don’t usually get rejected. They get forgotten.

A homeowner gets three quotes, gets busy, and goes quiet. Your team moves on to the next site visit. Nobody follows up the second, third or fourth time, because everyone is busy installing.

We take that follow-up off your plate. We aren’t a marketing agency, a lead-gen company or a call centre. We do one job: recover quotes you’ve already sent.

Who this is for

  • Install residential or light commercial solar in Gauteng, Western Cape or KwaZulu-Natal
  • Send roughly 25–100+ quotes or handle that many enquiries a month
  • Do about R3m–R15m a year in installations

Probably not a fit if you…

How it works

  1. 15-minute audit

    We look at your recent open and aged quotes together and show you where they’re going cold.

  2. Paper first

    Interested prospects are re-engaged or booked back into your sales process. You close them.

Questions installers ask

Will you talk to my customers as if you’re my company?

We follow up on your behalf, in a tone agreed with you, and hand interested prospects straight back to your team. We agree the wording and process with you before we start.

What do you need from me?

A list of open and aged quotes (name, contact, system quoted, date, value) and a named person we can hand appointments to. That’s it.

How do you measure the pilot?

Contact rate, replies, and appointments or meaningful re-engagements booked, plus whether you want to continue. We don’t judge it on closed revenue.

Is this the same as lead generation?

No. We don’t find new customers. We work the quotes you’ve already sent. New-lead follow-up may come later, only once the quote service is proven.

Why a 3-month commitment after the pilot?

Follow-up works through consistency over weeks, and solar decisions take time. Three months gives it a fair run.

Do you work outside Gauteng, Western Cape and KZN?

Our focus is those three provinces. Message us and we’ll tell you honestly whether we can help.
