Questions

Do you message my customers without asking me?

No. You approve every message first. We also ask for your written permission to contact your customers on your behalf. See the Messaging and approval policy.

Will this get me more sales?

We can’t promise that. It depends on your price, your quote and your customer. We promise every approved quote gets chased and you can see what happened.

Do you phone my customers?

No. We use WhatsApp and email only, and pass anyone who wants to talk to you.

What about discounts and technical questions?

We don’t answer for you. We record them, flag them in your report and message you straight away if urgent.

Why might a quote stay quiet after two follow-ups?

Property managers often wait on slow internal approvals. The report shows who is waiting and who has gone cold.

What happens after the pilot?

You see the report and decide: move to monthly, or stop with nothing further owed.

We send nothing to your customers until you approve the exact wording, by WhatsApp or email. Approval applies to the message and the quotes it covers. We re-confirm if wording changes materially.

By approving, you authorise us to send those messages on your behalf, and you confirm you have an existing business relationship with each recipient and a lawful reason to contact them about the quote they requested.

Only people who asked for or received your quote. We do not contact cold leads, buy lists, or anyone you tell us not to.

If a customer says stop, unsubscribe or similar, we stop at once, log it and tell you. We do not message them again.

Anything beyond a simple status (price pushback, technical questions, requests to talk, complaints) is logged and flagged to you. Urgent items are sent to you straight away.
