COMMON QUESTIONS
How long does a solar or battery install take?+

A battery and inverter install is usually done in one day. A full solar system with panels takes 2 to 4 days, depending on the roof. You get the timeline in your written quote.

Will my house keep running during load shedding?+

Yes, for the circuits you choose. The picker above sizes the system to what you want running. Heavy loads like the geyser, stove and aircon need a bigger system.

Do you charge for quotes or call-outs?+

The Load-shedding Survival Audit (R550) and Pre-Sale Electrical Audit (R550) are paid diagnostics — you get a full written report either way. Standard call-outs are priced upfront on the rate card before we dispatch, so there's never a surprise fee for someone showing up.

What if I'm not happy with the work?+

Every job carries a 12-month warranty on labour and parts. On membership plans, we back that with an explicit savings guarantee — Standard members save R1,500/year or the difference is refunded; Premium members save R3,000. .

How fast can you actually come out?+

Standard requests are handled by next day. Emergencies — no power, load-shedding faults, failed inspections before a deadline — are handled within 24 hours. Members and Estate Agent VIPs get faster tiers: 1-hour emergency response and 3-hour single-phase CoC turnaround respectively.

Are you licensed and insured?+

Yes — Red Seal and Wireman's licensed, with public liability cover up to R5,000,000. Certificates of Compliance are issued for every new or altered installation, as required by law.

Do prices include VAT?+

All prices shown exclude VAT. We'll confirm the VAT-inclusive total on your quote before any work is booked.

Can you do a CoC on the same day?+

Yes. Book before 10am for an expedited same-day CoC on a single-phase installation (R2,500 flat). If the installation fails, fixes are quoted before we start and the certificate is issued once it is compliant.

Do I need a CoC to add solar or a battery?+

Yes. Any new or altered electrical installation, including inverters, batteries and panels, needs a Certificate of Compliance. Your insurer and municipality may also ask for it. Every install we do includes one.

What if my CoC inspection fails?+

We list what failed and give a fixed price to correct it, often a DB board or surge protection upgrade. Nothing is done without your approval, and the certificate is issued once the installation is compliant.

What areas do you cover?+

Sandton, Bryanston, Fourways, Rosebank, Parktown, Randburg, Rivonia, Morningside, and building sites within 20km. Outside that radius, message us and we'll confirm if we can still help.

Certificate of Compliance (CoC) policy

1. What a CoC is

  • A Certificate of Compliance is a legal document, issued by a registered electrician, confirming that an electrical installation meets the electrical installation regulations at the time of inspection.
  • It is normally required when a property is sold, and for new or altered installations, including solar, inverters and batteries. Insurers may also ask for one.

2. Process and timelines

  • Standard single-phase: 2 to 3 days. 3-phase: 3 to 5 days.
  • Priority: 24 hours (single-phase), 48 hours (3-phase). Same-day expedited CoCs need booking before 10am, subject to inspection results.
  • VIP agents get a 3-hour single-phase CoC guarantee.
  • Same-day guarantee: single-phase, booked before 10am, with access on the day and an installation that passes inspection: certificate the same day or R250 off the CoC fee. If it fails, we give you the defect list and fixed price straight away, and the guarantee does not apply to time spent on fixes.

3. If the installation fails

  • We give you a written list of defects and a fixed price to correct them. Nothing is done without your approval.
  • A certificate is only issued once the installation is compliant. We cannot certify an unsafe installation for any price or deadline.
  • Common fixes: old or overcrowded DB boards, missing earth leakage or surge protection, exposed wiring, faulty plugs.

4. Re-inspection

  • If a buyer or their inspector flags a fault after our certificate, we re-inspect free within 30 days. After 30 days, re-inspection is R350 (free for VIP agents). Fixing a defect we should have caught at our inspection is free, within the 12-month warranty and if the installation has not been altered since.

5. Solar, inverter and battery CoCs

  • Every solar, inverter or battery install we do includes a CoC. Solar-ready CoCs are available from R2,200 if you are adding equipment.
  • Grid-tied systems may need registration with your municipality or utility as a small-scale embedded generator (SSEG). We prepare the documents with you, but approval sits with the authority, and timing is outside our control.

6. Your responsibilities

  • Tell us about all alterations, extensions and other electricians who worked on the property.
  • Keep the certificate safe. It stays valid only for the installation as inspected; later changes need a new certificate.
