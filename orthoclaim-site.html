Why I built OrthoClaim.

I'm Callum. I'm not a surgeon and I'm not a biller. I got tired of watching small ortho practices lose money they'd already earned.

I spent time looking at how private practices actually get paid in South Africa. The pattern was the same everywhere. A procedure is done. A claim goes in. The scheme pays late, pays short, or denies it outright. The practice is too busy treating patients to chase R45 short-pays or appeal a pre-auth denial, so the money is written off, forgotten or absorbed.

I built OrthoClaim to be the one who chases it. Not a billing bureau and not a software product: a recovery service that does one thing, getting back what the schemes already owe you, and only charges when we recover it.

I'm bringing in billing specialists who know ortho coding, scheme rules and pre-auth appeals. I'm the accountability layer and they bring the expertise. You get both.

Callum Denysschen
Founder, OrthoClaim

Pre-auth denials are where the money is lost.

A major ortho procedure can be delayed or cancelled at pre-auth: R30k–R150k lost per denial. Most practices write it off and reschedule. We appeal the denial, resubmit with correct coding and recover the fee. If the patient is already booked, we rescue the pre-auth before surgery.
