What we install

The Patient Revenue Recovery System

Five parts, one goal: recovered appointments. AI sits underneath. Workflows, integrations, reporting and your team sit on top.

A

24/7 enquiry response

Website and WhatsApp enquiries get an instant reply. The assistant answers approved FAQs, identifies the enquiry type, collects basic details, routes it correctly and moves the patient toward a booking.

B

Missed-call recovery

Nobody answered? Within a controlled timeframe the patient gets a message and the enquiry becomes a conversation instead of a lost call.

"Hi, this is your dental practice. We noticed we missed your call. How can we help?"
C

Abandoned-enquiry recovery

Someone asks about implants, gets an answer, then goes quiet. An approved, polite follow-up sequence brings them back. Never aggressive, never endless.

Day 1: helpful follow-upDay 3: answer / FAQDay 7: final check-in

Stops immediately if the patient opts out.

D

Cancellation and no-show recovery

A cancellation stops being lost revenue and becomes a slot to fill. The vacancy is flagged, your waiting list is notified, a suitable appointment is offered, then confirmed and reminded.

E

Patient reactivation

Approved recall campaigns for patients who haven't been back in 6, 12, 18 or 24 months. Often the biggest untapped pool in the practice.

Only with proper consent or lawful basis, opt-outs, data minimisation and practice-approved messaging. Health information is special personal information under POPIA, so we never improvise here.

+

Measured and optimised

A revenue-recovery dashboard shows what actually happened. Monthly optimisation improves the workflows. You see results in business terms, not message counts.

Safe by design

The assistant never gives medical advice.

A hard boundary between administration and clinical care. When confidence is low, the assistant hands over to your team instead of guessing.

The assistant handles
  • Administrative questions and practice information
  • Booking, rescheduling and cancellation
  • Opening hours and location
  • Approved pricing information
  • Basic process explanations
  • Routing to the right person
Your team handles
  • Diagnosis and treatment recommendations
  • Emergencies and anyone in pain
  • Clinical interpretation
  • Complaints and sensitive situations
  • Unusual medical questions
  • Anyone who asks to speak to a person
Always transparent: patients are told they're talking to an automated assistant. "Hi, I'm the practice's virtual assistant. I can help with appointments and general practice information. For clinical questions, I'll connect you with the practice team."
