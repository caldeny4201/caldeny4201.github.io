Questions, answered

Do I need to be home?

Usually not. We need access and any hazards noted. Photos are sent when done.

Will cleaning void my warranty?

We use soft brushes and purified water in line with common manufacturer guidance. Check your warranty terms if unsure; we'll help.

How often should I clean?

Most Joburg roofs benefit from 2–4 cleans a year depending on trees, roads and birds.

Do you install or repair?

We focus on maintenance, cleaning, checks and reporting. Faults are documented and referred to a qualified electrician or your installer.

Which areas?

Johannesburg and surrounds incl. Sandton, Randburg, Fourways, Midrand, Roodepoort and the East Rand.

What about bad weather?

We don't clean in heavy wind or lightning. We reschedule free.
