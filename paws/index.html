Swap or refund

Box arrives warm?

Questions

Before you join

How fast do you reply?

Every WhatsApp message is answered by a person who works here. Usually within 30 minutes, and always within 24 hours, every day of the week. No bots and no ticket numbers.

When do you open?

We have not fixed a date yet. Everyone on the waitlist hears first, on WhatsApp, with time to claim founding pricing before the public does.

Does joining the waitlist cost anything?

No. We take no payment and no card details. You only send us a WhatsApp message with your details.

Where will you deliver?

We plan to launch with Sandton, Bryanston, Fourways, Rosebank, Dainfern and Steyn City, in temperature-controlled vans. More areas follow once the cold chain is proven. See .

Do you make food for cats?

Yes. Cats have their own plan, priced from R690 a month, and the same trial box and swap guarantee.

Is raw food safe?

Fresh and gently cooked meals are handled and packed under cold-chain controls, and every box comes with storage and thawing guidance. Talk to your vet before changing a diet, especially for puppies, seniors and pets with health conditions.

How does the referral code work?

You get a code when you join. A friend enters it on the waitlist form. Once they place and pay for a first order, you both get R100 off and you move up the founder ladder: a numbered founder card, your name on the wall, a recipe named after your pet and more. Full terms are in the .

Are the premium services available yet?

Not yet. Concierge, bespoke nutrition, Founders' Circle and the other services listed above are planned. Prices are indicative and may change. We are collecting interest on the waitlist first, and we will message you before anything goes live.

Can I visit the kitchen?

Yes. The Biome Bar is open-format behind glass, and the Gear Studio and Apothecary are walk-in. The hydro-suite is by appointment.
